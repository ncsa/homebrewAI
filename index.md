## What Have You Built With AI?

In the 1970s, a group of hobbyists gathered to share their homemade computer projects. They called it the **Homebrew Computer Club**.

Today, we are living through another transformative moment.

**Welcome to Homebrew AI Club**: a community for staff exploring, building, testing, and sharing practical applications of Generative AI.

---

## Bring What You Are Working On

We welcome demonstrations and discussions of:

- Copilot agents and assistants
- AI-powered workflows
- Automated reports and documents
- Custom GPTs
- Research and productivity tools
- Prompt libraries
- Proofs of concept
- Experiments that failed but taught valuable lessons

---

## How It Works

### Show & Tell
